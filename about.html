About C & E Logistics

Our Story

C & E Logistics was founded on a simple idea: freight shipping should be reliable, transparent, and stress-free. For over 15 years, we've helped businesses of all sizes move their goods safely and on schedule, building long-term partnerships along the way.

Today, our fleet and warehouse network cover routes across the region, backed by a dispatch team that's available around the clock. Whether you're shipping a single pallet or coordinating a full truckload, we treat your freight like it's our own.

What We Value

Reliability

On-time delivery isn't a goal, it's the standard we hold ourselves to on every shipment.

Transparency

Clear communication and honest pricing, with no hidden surprises along the way.

Partnership

We work as an extension of your team, not just a vendor you call when something ships.
